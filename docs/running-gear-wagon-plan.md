# Running Gear Wagon Plan

A simple flatbed on a running gear for fence work: hauling old posts, scrap wire, logs, T-posts and wire rolls. Used with the [hog fence plan](feral_hog_perimeter_fence_plan.md).

**Overall size:** 14 ft long x 6.5 ft wide deck, 12 to 16 in. side boards, 3.5 ft headboard.

## Frame
- **Main rails (2):** 14 ft of 4" channel or 3"x2"x3/16" rectangular tube, set 5.5 to 6 ft apart.
- **Cross members (8 to 9):** 2"x2"x1/8" tube or 2" angle, 6.5 ft long, every 20 to 24 in. Weld them flush to the top of the rails.
- **Outer edge rails (2):** 2"x2" tube, 14 ft, welded to the cross member ends to tie it all together and give the stake pockets something to attach to.
- **Cross brace:** two diagonal braces from the tongue end back to the rails so the frame doesn't rack.

## Running gear
**Decision: buy a used ag running gear and rebuild it.** Building one was considered ([chassis build plan](wagon-chassis-build-plan.html), [build notes](wagon-running-gear-build-notes.md)) and set aside as too much work.

- **Models:** John Deere 953, 963, 1065; IH / McCormick-Deering; Kory, H&S, Pequea, Electric Wheel. Retired 1,000-gal NH3 nurse-tank gears are also a good fit (cut off the tank cradles).
- **Size:** 6–8 ton, automotive steering, adjustable reach pole, 6-bolt hubs common.
- **Price targets:** $150–600 at online auction (AuctionTime, BigIron sold results) for one that needs work; dealer asking prices of $650–3,500 are mostly markup. Budget $300–800 for rebuild parts.
- **Walk away if:** the axle beam or reach pole is bent, welds are cracked, or the steering spindles are seized.
- **Rebuild:** wheel bearings, races and seals; kingpin bushings; tie-rod ends; tires rated ~1,750 lb+ each.
- **Axles:** 2 × 3,500 lb minimum. No brakes needed on-farm; keep the loaded wagon lighter than the tractor.

## Deck
- 2x8 oak or treated boards, 6.5 ft long, laid crosswise on the cross members with a 1/4" gap. That's about 20 boards. Bolt with 3/8" carriage bolts or lag screws, or use self-tapping deck screws on steel.

## Headboard
- Two 2"x2" tube uprights, 3.5 ft tall, at the front corners, with 3 horizontal rails between them and a few vertical 1" pipes to stop wire rolls from pushing through. Gusset the bottom of the uprights to the frame.

## Sides
- 10 stake pockets (5 per side) welded to the edge rails, taking 2x6 or 2x4 stakes.
- 2x6 side boards, 1 or 2 high. Leave the ends open so long posts can slide through.
- 6 to 8 welded D-rings or loops along the edges for ratchet straps.

## Hitch and safety
- Drawbar or tongue with a 1" hitch pin hole (or a pintle eye), plus safety chains.
- If you'll cross a public road, add a slow-moving-vehicle emblem and reflectors.

## Rough parts list
- Steel: about 14 ft x 2 rails, 14 ft x 2 edge rails, 6.5 ft x 9 cross members, plus headboard tube and braces (roughly 150 to 180 ft total).
- Running gear: used wagon gear or tandem axle set, with springs, hubs, and tires.
- Lumber: about 20 deck boards (2x8x6.5'), 4 side boards (2x6x14').
- Hardware: stake pockets, D-rings, carriage bolts, hitch pin, chains.
