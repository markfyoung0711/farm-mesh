Fence Wagon Build Plan

Chassis only: a welded steel frame on two 3,500 lb trailer axles, with a turntable front axle and a hinged tongue for a PTO-and-hitch-lift tractor. The deck and side boards come later and bolt on.

How it goes together

The frame is the backbone, so no separate reach pole is needed. The rear axle bolts solid under the rear bolster. The front axle sits on its own lower bolster and swivels under the frame on a turntable around a center kingbolt. The tongue hinges on the front axle so it can rise and fall with the tractor drawbar.

Build order

  1. Set the two main rails on stands, 36" apart on center, and square them by measuring the diagonals.
  2. Weld the upper front bolster under the rails 2 ft from the front, and the rear bolster plus spacer 2 ft from the rear. Check for a ~10 ft wheelbase.
  3. Weld the 9 cross members on top of the rails, then the edge rails to their ends. Add the X braces between the main rails.
  4. Weld the kingbolt pad to the upper bolster and drill the 1-1/4" kingbolt hole on the frame centerline.
  5. Build the front axle assembly: kingbolt pad and hinge ears on the lower bolster, U-bolt the axle under it, bolt the turntable on top.
  6. Raise the frame, set it on the turntable, and drop in the kingbolt with a grease zerk.
  7. U-bolt the rear axle under the rear spacer, centered and square to the frame.
  8. Build the tongue A-frame and hitch plate; pin it to the hinge ears. Weld the steering stops at ~35°.
  9. Weld the D-rings along the edge rails.
  10. Mount the wheels and tires, grease everything, prime and paint.
  11. Test empty: full-lock turns both ways to check tire clearance, then a light load. Retighten the U-bolts and kingbolt after the first few trips.

Loads

Rated: two 3,500 lb axles carry 7,000 lb gross. Design for about 5,000 lb of cargo plus the ~1,000 lb chassis.

Without brakes: keep the loaded wagon lighter than the tractor pulling it. Gear down before slopes.

Public roads: Texas generally wants brakes over 4,500 lb gross. Keep it on the farm or check the rules first.

Upgrade path: if you later find a junk running gear with automotive steering, its front axle can replace the turntable assembly. Bolt its bolster to the same upper bolster.

Sizes are planning estimates for a slow, on-farm wagon. Have a welder check the steel sizes and welds before loading it heavy, and confirm tire clearance with the real axles before welding the cross members.
